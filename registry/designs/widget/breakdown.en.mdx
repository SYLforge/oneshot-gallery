# WIDGET — breakdown

## The hook
A spatial dashboard in visionOS glass-futurism style — a constellation of eight
translucent widgets floating at different depths over an aurora ground, tilting
toward the pointer like held glass. Where HANJI SLATE is a single e-ink device
exploding, WIDGET is a field of glass suspended in 3D space. Pure code, no
images.

## Page structure, top to bottom
Hero (display title and Korean subtitle over the depth gradient); the spatial
stage (the signature — eight widgets lean toward the pointer by depth, an
aurora drifting subtly behind); and the colophon.

## Signature — the 3D widget stage
`usePointerParallax` attaches to the stage and leans each `[data-tilt]` widget
in 3D toward the pointer. A single rAF-coalesced loop lerps (0.12) every card
each frame and writes four custom properties (`--w-rx/--w-ry/--w-lift`) straight
onto it. Each card declares `data-depth` ∈ {near, mid, far} — a near card tilts
most and lifts furthest, a far card barely moves, so the field reads as real
parallax depth. `perspective: 1200px` + `transform-style: preserve-3d` carry
the depth; a contact drop-shadow on each card grounds the float as suspension
in space, not flat translateZ. At rest the loop parks itself — zero cost.

## Techniques
1. **pointer-parallax** — widgets lean toward the pointer at depth-scaled rates
   (a single rAF lerp loop measures each card's rect, scales by a depth
   multiplier, and writes `--w-rx/ry/lift` — ported from aurora's proven hook).
2. **spatial-depth** — `data-depth` near/mid/far decides tilt, lift, and contact
   shadow strength together. The visionOS spatial cue.
3. **glass-surface** — `backdrop-filter: blur(12px) saturate(160%)`,
   `rgba(255,255,255,0.12)` surface, multi-layer box-shadow (bevel + ambient +
   key + contact) plus a separate contact drop-shadow.
4. **clip-path-reveal** — widgets wipe in by clip-path on scroll, staggered 70ms
   in depth order (far → near).

## The eight widgets, by depth
Weather and Activity float near (lift most); Schedule, Now-playing, and Timer
ride mid; Weekly-chart, Air & sun, and Messages sit far. Each carries bilingual
Korean + English data, and every figure uses `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`
so columns align.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: the parallax hook never starts — every widget stands flat as a
complete, readable dashboard. No-JS: without `.widget-js` the pre-reveal hidden
state never applies, so all eight widgets ship as a finished, source-ordered
dashboard. AA/AAA contrast over glass (body 15.4:1, secondary 8.4:1; the iris
accent is large/decorative only and annotated as such). Korean-first bilingual,
with `:lang(ko)` carrying its own line-height and letter-spacing.
