# ORBIT — breakdown

## The hook
The gallery's first `three-d-immersive` entry. A fictional studio drops one
sneaker silhouette at a time; the signature sneaker sits on a turntable in a
seamless studio — drag to orbit it 360°. Built **vanilla** (no three.js) per
the zero-dependency policy: a canvas 2.5D sprite turntable.

## Renderer choice
Canvas 2.5D sprite-turntable — pre-rendered angles swapped on drag with
smooth interpolation, plus specular hint and turntable shadow. Chosen over
raw WebGL for reliability. Enough angles to feel continuous.

## Techniques
1. **pointer-parallax** — drag-orbit the product with inertia.
2. **scroll-scrub-pinned** — pinned "the build" exploded-view section.
3. **clip-path-reveal** — colorway/feature panels wipe in.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: product static at hero angle, no orbit inertia. No-JS: static
product hero. AA contrast. Korean-first bilingual.
