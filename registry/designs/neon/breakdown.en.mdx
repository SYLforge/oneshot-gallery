# NEON — breakdown

## The hook
A cyberpunk street at 3 AM — rain-slick neon signs, holographic storefronts,
retrowave grid. Where GRADIENT-PLAZA is an open vaporwave mall under the sky,
NEON is a claustrophobic vertical city in the rain. Pure code, no images.

## Techniques
1. **crt-scanline** — neon glow flicker + scanline overlay + animated rain
   (diagonal lines). Photosensitive-safe (under 3Hz, off under reduced motion).
2. **marquee** — holographic storefront tickers scrolling at varying speeds.
3. **ascii-render** — neon signs (라면, 술, 전자) as glowing ASCII block letters
   in text-shadow stacks.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: rain static, signs steady. No-JS: fully readable. AA contrast.
Korean-first bilingual.
