# NEON — breakdown

## The hook
A cyberpunk street at 3 AM in 2087 — rain-slick neon signs, holographic
storefront tickers, vertical signs hanging off building faces, light bleeding
into puddles, and a retrowave grid where the city ends. Where GRADIENT-PLAZA
is an open vaporwave mall under the sky, NEON is a claustrophobic vertical
city in the rain. Not a single raster pixel.

## Scene structure
Top to bottom — a title bleeding through rain; a street scene of building
silhouettes, hanging signs, and a puddle whose reflections drift to your
pointer; a bilingual holographic ticker; an ASCII neon sign gallery
(ramen · bar · electronics · barber); a retrowave grid-and-sun at the
vanishing horizon; and a short poem that is the city's voice.

## Techniques
1. **crt-scanline** — neon glow flicker + scanline overlay + animated
   diagonal rain (a repeating gradient). The flicker keyframes run on
   mutually-prime periods (3.2s / 4.1s / 5.7s) so they never synchronize and
   the street reads as alive. Photosensitive-safe (under 3Hz, fully stopped
   under reduced motion).
2. **marquee** — a holographic storefront ticker scrolling at 30s linear.
   Korean rides in magenta, English in cyan; the track is doubled so it
   loops without a seam.
3. **ascii-render** — the neon signs (라면, 술, 전자, 이발) are glowing ASCII
   block letters in monospace, lit by stacked text-shadows. Each sign carries
   its own flicker period keyed to its color.
4. **pointer-parallax** — the normalized pointer (-0.5→0.5) is written to
   `--neon-px` / `--neon-py`. Buildings drift at depth-tiered 8–20px offsets,
   hanging signs lift vertically, and puddle reflections slide sideways — all
   from two CSS custom properties. It is pure input→style mapping with no
   animation loop, so under reduced motion the scene simply composes at rest.

## The retrowave grid
The horizon section is a floor on a 320px perspective, rotated 70° and
scrolling toward the viewer every 4s in cyan+magenta. The sun above it is a
circular magenta→amber→purple gradient sliced by a horizontal-slat mask — the
sun from an '80s album cover, drawn entirely in CSS.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: rain, flicker, and grid-scroll all stop; signs stay steadily
lit. No-JS: every line of text is a readable static list. AA contrast — every
neon hue clears 5:1 on rain-black. Korean-first bilingual: the poem and the
signs let Korean speak first.
