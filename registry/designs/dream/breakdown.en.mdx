# DREAM — breakdown

## The hook
A sleep house whose page drifts like the moment before sleep. Pastel clouds
float in layered depth toward the pointer; a pinned descent dissolves them as
you scroll deeper; a soft haze overlays everything. Where SUP is grounded
(grass, growth), DREAM is airborne (clouds, drift) — the wellness vertical's
weightless side.

## Techniques
1. **pointer-parallax** — cloud/bubble SVG layers drift toward pointer at
   different depths (weightless floating).
2. **scroll-scrub-pinned** — a pinned "descent into sleep" where clouds slowly
   dissolve as you scroll.
3. **feturbulence-texture** — soft cloud/noise haze overlay (the dream fog).

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: clouds static at a composed arrangement. No-JS: fully readable.
AA contrast (dark text on pastel). Korean-first bilingual.
