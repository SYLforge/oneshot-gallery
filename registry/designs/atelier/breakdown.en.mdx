# ATELIER — breakdown

## The hook
A high-fashion maison publishing the RESERVE collection as an editorial
lookbook — the magazine-cover-as-website. Pure black/off-white + one rare
antique-gold accent. Where ONDO is a single perfume bottle, ATELIER is a full
collection — oversized Bodoni, expensive silence.

## Techniques
1. **char-split-reveal** — the maison masthead reveals letter-by-letter in
   oversized Bodoni serif (the cover masthead).
2. **scroll-scrub-pinned** — a lookbook section where looks crossfade as you
   scroll, like turning editorial pages.
3. **clip-path-reveal** — editorial sections wipe in with hairline cuts.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: static editorial spread. No-JS: fully readable lookbook.
AA contrast. Korean-first bilingual.
