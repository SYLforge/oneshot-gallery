# GLITCH — breakdown

## The hook
A VFX and experimental-music studio that treats failure as a finish —
datamoshed type, RGB-split wordmarks, scanline and noise overlays, and
corrupted ASCII that scrambles periodically. Where PALE.SIGNAL is clean
phosphor terminal poetry, GLITCH is corrupted, broken-beautiful.

## Distinction from PALE.SIGNAL
PALE.SIGNAL = clean phosphor green / typewriter / log poetry.
GLITCH = corrupted / RGB-split / datamosh / broken hardware as intention.
Opposite registers within the same family.

## Techniques
1. **crt-scanline** — heavy scanline + RGB-shift + noise (aggressive; the
   glitch aesthetic). Photosensitive-safe (under 3Hz, off under reduced motion).
2. **clip-path-reveal** — sections reveal with jagged/glitchy clip-path tears.
3. **ascii-render** — corrupted ASCII art / glitch glyphs that scramble
   periodically.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: no glitch animation, static. No-JS: fully readable. AA
contrast (RGB colors verified; decorative where they fall short). Korean-first
bilingual. Photosensitive safety: all flicker under 3Hz, removed under
reduced motion.
