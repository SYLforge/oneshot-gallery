# CHAPTER — breakdown

## The hook
The Book of Hours — a digital book that sets a day as six hours, each hour a
chapter. The third editorial-serif entry, built as the book-as-object: a
frontispiece, six pinned spreads that crossfade like turning a page as you
scroll, and a publisher's colophon. Visually distinct from YEOBAEK (the quiet
airy literary voice) and ZINE (the loud photocopied collage).

## Signature moment
The page genuinely *turns*. The spreads section is `(CHAPTERS + 1) × 100vh`
tall; its inner sticky stage holds the book open while you scroll. As you
scroll, the hook writes `data-spreadstate` (0..5) and a smoothed
`--chapter-spread` (0→1); exactly one spread matches the state at a time, so
the crossfade is a clean opacity + hairline-scale dissolve — never a layout
property, never fragile float math. The editorial frame never moves; only the
leaf changes.

## Content
Six chapters — 새벽(Dawn), 아침(Morning), 정오(Noon), 오후(Afternoon),
황혼(Dusk), 밤(Night) — each 4–6 sentences of literary Korean prose with a
drop-cap lead and a unique marginalia note (the reader's annotation, no
longer identical across chapters).

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: static (the crossfade stays live — it tracks the wheel 1:1;
only the smoothing is bypassed, because it is a reveal of already-present
content, not motion). No-JS: fully readable — every spread, marginalia, and
the colophon are in the DOM in source order. AA contrast on every ink token;
the decorative red is large/ornamental only. Korean-first bilingual;
`:lang(ko)` gets its own line-height (1.9) and keep-all breaking.

## Craft
All 12 checklist items applied: per-token contrast comments, display
`letter-spacing: -0.04em`, `:lang(ko)` line-height 1.9, two+ purpose-baked
easings (page-turn / hover), `clamp()` type scale, 4px spacing tokens,
multi-layer box-shadow on the marginalia, three media queries (720/900/1200),
reduced-motion fallbacks, no `transition: all`, `:focus-visible`, and
`text-wrap: pretty` on the prose.
