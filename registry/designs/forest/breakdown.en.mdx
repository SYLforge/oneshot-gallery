# FOREST — breakdown

## The hook
A forest retreat where the canopy darkens as you descend. A real painterly
canopy photograph hangs behind CSS leaf shapes and a feTurbulence dapple, so
the retreat reads as a place — not a gradient. The third organic-nature entry,
visually distinct from SUP (water surface) and DREAM (soft pastel haze).

## The page, top to bottom
The hero under a drifting canopy; the descent — a sticky section that
darkens with scroll progress, three lines of Korean marking the deepening
(Light thins · moss thickens · breath deepens); the layers of the canopy —
a section naming the three depths (far / mid / near); the understory —
quiet cards for moss, fern, and fungi; the dappled-leaf overlay across
everything; and the darkened footer where the forest holds its breath.

## Techniques
1. **scroll-scrub-pinned** — the descent. A sticky inner reads
   `--forest-scrub` (0→1, lerp-bypassed under reduced motion) and composes
   its background and text color from it via `color-mix(in oklab)`: the
   further you scroll, the deeper the canopy becomes.
2. **feturbulence-texture** — a full-bleed SVG `feTurbulence` fractalNoise
   pass, low-frequency, masked into a leaf-dapple color matrix and laid over
   the page with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`. It is the moving light through
   leaves, and it retreats as you descend.
3. **pointer-parallax** — three canopy depths drift toward the pointer at
   different magnitudes (the far photograph barely sways, the mid clusters
   carry the drift, the near leaves move most), driven by `--forest-px` /
   `--forest-py`. Each depth also sways on its own mutually-prime period
   (13s / 17s / 23s) so the canopy never loops. Pure input→style; under
   reduced motion the layers simply sit.

## The canopy photograph
The far layer is a single generated image (ComfyUI, Illustrious
bismuthIllustrious_v80, 1536×640), darkened to 42% and saturation-reduced so
it reads as atmosphere behind the code-drawn leaves — a hybrid of raster
depth and vector shape. See meta.json provenance.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: the parallax stops and the descent tracks scroll without
smoothing — a complete, composed static retreat. No-JS: every line is a
readable static page. AA contrast (the darkened descent holds text contrast
against the deepening green). Korean-first bilingual.
