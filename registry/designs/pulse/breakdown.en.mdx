# PULSE — breakdown

## The hook
A fictional record label that drops one release into the dark at a time. The
signature is a generative particle nebula — a few hundred 3D-projected points
on canvas 2D that pulse to a simulated beat. Where ORBIT is a product you
control (turntable), PULSE is generative and autonomous (breathing nebula).

## Renderer
Vanilla canvas 2D with manual 3D perspective projection (multiply points by a
perspective matrix by hand — a few hundred points, cheap). NO three.js — the
gallery's zero-dependency policy.

## Techniques
1. **canvas-particles** — the 3D-projected nebula pulsing to a simulated
   ~120bpm beat (no real audio).
2. **pointer-parallax** — the nebula's camera tilts subtly with pointer.
3. **scroll-scrub-pinned** — a pinned tracklist where the nebula shifts
   color/intensity per track.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: nebula static at a composed frame. No-JS: a static nebula hero.
AA contrast. Korean-first bilingual.
