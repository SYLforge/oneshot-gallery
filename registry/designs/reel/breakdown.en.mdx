# REEL — breakdown

## The hook
A wedding/film photographer's portfolio as a contact sheet come to life. Warm
Kodak-toned CSS-gradient stills develop under scroll, light leaks sweep, grain
overlays. Where HALFLIGHT is cold procedural scrubbed reels, REEL is warm
analog film — the editorial-photography look.

## Techniques
1. **scroll-scrub-pinned** — a pinned contact-sheet section where stills
   "develop" (fade in) as you scroll, like revealing a contact sheet.
2. **clip-path-reveal** — stills reveal with film-strip sprocket-hole wipes.
3. **crt-scanline** — animated film grain + periodic light-leak sweep (the
   analog projector texture, not a CRT).

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: grain static, stills visible. No-JS: fully readable portfolio.
AA contrast. Korean-first bilingual.
