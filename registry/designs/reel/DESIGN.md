# REEL — design spec

## Identity
아날로그 필름 릭 사진 — a wedding/film photographer's portfolio as a contact
sheet come to life. Warm Kodak-toned CSS-gradient stills develop under scroll,
light leaks sweep, grain overlays. Distinct from HALFLIGHT (cold procedural
scrubbed reels): REEL is warm analog film — the editorial-photography look.

## Distinction from HALFLIGHT
HALFLIGHT = cold / procedural / scrubbed-reel / digital-dark.
REEL = warm / analog / film-leak / contact-sheet / Kodak.

## Palette
Amber `#d4a554`, faded red `#c44830`, deep brown-black `#1a120e`, light-leak
orange `#ff8844`. CSS-gradient "stills" (no real images).

## Techniques
1. scroll-scrub-pinned — contact-sheet stills develop with scroll.
2. clip-path-reveal — sprocket-hole film-strip wipes.
3. crt-scanline — film grain + light-leak sweep.

## Accessibility
Reduced motion: grain static, stills visible. No-JS: fully readable. AA
contrast. Korean-first bilingual.
