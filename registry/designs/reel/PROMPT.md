# REEL — reproducible prompt

## Distilled recipe
A wedding/film photographer's portfolio as a contact sheet come to life — warm
Kodak-toned CSS-gradient "stills" that develop under scroll, light-leak sweeps,
and a grain overlay. Distinct from HALFLIGHT (the 1st cinematic-dark):
HALFLIGHT is cold procedural scrubbed reels; REEL is warm analog film stills
with light leaks — the editorial-photography look.

## Techniques
1. `scroll-scrub-pinned` — contact-sheet stills develop (fade in) as you scroll.
2. `clip-path-reveal` — film-strip sprocket-hole wipe reveals.
3. `crt-scanline` — film grain + light-leak sweep (analog texture).
